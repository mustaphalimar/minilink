Short links with expiry, analytics and Redis caching

Create short URLs, share them, and inspect click stats from one screen. Built with Spring Boot, Angular and Redis.

Cached redirects
Resolved destinations are cached in Redis with a TTL.
Rate limited
Link creation is limited per IP, per minute and per hour.
Auto cleanup
Expired links are purged by a scheduled job.
